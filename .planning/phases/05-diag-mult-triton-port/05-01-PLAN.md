| nn.Parameter → diag_mult | `subdiag` and `v` originate from `nn.Parameter` (training) or forward-call results (inference). Not user-controlled HTTP/CLI input. |
| Environment variable (`TORCH_STRUCTURED_BACKEND`) → resolver | Existing Phase 4 mitigation: validated against the exact set `{triton, cuda, torch, auto}`; `ValueError` on anything else (T-04-01). No new attack surface in Phase 5. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-01 | Tampering | `_triton/diag_mult/op.py` wrapper boundary — non-contiguous complex tensor passed in | mitigate | `assert v.is_contiguous()` and `assert subdiag.is_contiguous()` before `torch.view_as_real` (Pitfall 3); fires loudly with `AssertionError` at the wrapper before kernel launch |
